## 📜 **Western Philosophy Timeline**

---

### 🏛️ **Ancient Philosophy (c. 600 BCE – 500 CE)**

**1. Pre-Socratics** (c. 600–400 BCE)

- **[[Thales of Miletus]], Heraclitus, Parmenides, Anaximander, Pythagoras**  
    _Explored nature, being, and the origins of the cosmos. Birth of rational inquiry._
    

**2. Classical Greece** (c. 470–322 BCE)

- **Socrates (470–399 BCE)** — Socratic method, ethics as a form of knowledge.
    
- **Plato (427–347 BCE)** — Theory of Forms, idealism, the Republic (justice, the state).
    
- **Aristotle (384–322 BCE)** — Logic, ethics (virtue), politics, biology, metaphysics.
    

**3. Hellenistic Philosophy** (c. 300 BCE – 200 CE)

- **Epicurus** — Atomism, hedonism (Epicureanism).
    
- **Zeno of Citium** — Founder of Stoicism (reason, virtue, nature).
    
- **Plotinus (204–270 CE)** — Neoplatonism, mystical one-ness.
    

---

### ⛪ **Medieval Philosophy (c. 500–1500 CE)**

**4. Early Christian & Scholastic**

- **Augustine of Hippo (354–430)** — Integrated Platonism with Christianity.
    
- **Thomas Aquinas (1225–1274)** — Synthesized Aristotle with Christian doctrine, natural theology (Scholasticism).
    
- **Anselm of Canterbury** — Ontological argument for God’s existence.
    

---

### ✨ **Renaissance & Early Modern Philosophy (c. 1500–1800 CE)**

**5. Renaissance Humanism**

- **Erasmus, Pico della Mirandola** — Emphasis on human dignity, classical learning.
    

**6. Rationalism & Empiricism**

- **René Descartes (1596–1650)** — “Cogito ergo sum,” mind-body dualism, methodical doubt.
    
- **Baruch Spinoza (1632–1677)** — Pantheism, ethics as geometry.
    
- **Gottfried Leibniz (1646–1716)** — Monadology, pre-established harmony.
    
- **John Locke (1632–1704)** — Empiricism, tabula rasa, political liberalism.
    
- **George Berkeley (1685–1753)** — Idealism, to be is to be perceived.
    
- **David Hume (1711–1776)** — Radical empiricism, skepticism, causality critique.
    

**7. Enlightenment**

- **Immanuel Kant (1724–1804)** — Synthetic a priori, Critique of Pure Reason, moral autonomy.
    

---

### 🏭 **19th Century Philosophy**

**8. German Idealism & Reaction**

- **Georg Wilhelm Friedrich Hegel (1770–1831)** — Dialectics, absolute idealism.
    
- **Arthur Schopenhauer (1788–1860)** — The Will as metaphysical force.
    
- **Søren Kierkegaard (1813–1855)** — Existentialism, faith vs. reason.
    

**9. Marxism**

- **Karl Marx (1818–1883)** — Historical materialism, critique of capitalism.
    

**10. Positivism & Pragmatism**

- **Auguste Comte (1798–1857)** — Sociology, Positivism.
    
- **Charles Sanders Peirce (1839–1914)** — Pragmatism, semiotics.
    
- **William James (1842–1910)** — Pragmatism, psychology, radical empiricism.
    

**11. Existentialism & Nihilism**

- **Friedrich Nietzsche (1844–1900)** — Critique of morality, will to power, “God is dead.”
    

---

### 💭 **20th Century & Contemporary Philosophy**

**12. Phenomenology & Existentialism**

- **Edmund Husserl (1859–1938)** — Founder of phenomenology.
    
- **Martin Heidegger (1889–1976)** — Being and Time, ontology, existentialism.
    
- **Jean-Paul Sartre (1905–1980)** — Existentialism, radical freedom.
    
- **Simone de Beauvoir (1908–1986)** — Existential feminism.
    

**13. Analytic Philosophy & Language**

- **Bertrand Russell (1872–1970)** — Logic, analytic philosophy.
    
- **Ludwig Wittgenstein (1889–1951)** — Language games, meaning as use.
    

**14. Critical Theory & Postmodernism**

- **Theodor Adorno, Max Horkheimer** — Frankfurt School, cultural critique.
    
- **Michel Foucault (1926–1984)** — Power/knowledge, discourse, genealogy.
    
- **Jacques Derrida (1930–2004)** — Deconstruction.
