## The First Philosopher (or In di beninging)

I've asked myself and various [[Z_sources]] and found that most lists name [[Thales of Miletus]] as the first western philosopher. According to Wikipedia he was the first to challenge the belief to explain the world with mythology but instead to try and explain the world with science. Numbers, formulas et. al. 
~~I don't want to go deep into the facts but a general knowledge can't hurt.~~ 

During the Bronze Age Miletus already prospered as a major center of trade, culture and innovation. It laid right at the shores so naturally the Milesians were involved in trading over sea and land. The city thrived upon that and was known for it's riches, naval warfare, and grid of colonies. No wonder some people (probably rich) got bored enough to start writing.

> Though now that I think about it: Is it the happy that write? Is it the happy that think? Is it the happy that complain and whine and ask questions?


Anyway, the people who started asking these questions founded the Milesian school which consists of [[Thales of Miletus]] [[Anaximander of Miletus]] and Anaximenes.
